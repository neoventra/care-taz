About this service

Essential domestic help — nothing more, nothing less.

Housekeeping services from Caretaz Healthcare focus on the practical work of keeping a home clean and organised. Cleaning, laundry and shopping take energy — our visits return that energy to living, resting and spending time with people who matter.

This pathway is domestic help, not personal care. If washing, dressing or medication support is also needed, we can pair housekeeping with care visits under the same Nottingham provider — so homes and people both stay well supported.

Families choose us for reliable schedules, DBS-checked team members and the reassurance of a CQC-registered organisation behind every visit.

Good to know

Questions families ask first

Straight answers before you book.

Is housekeeping the same as personal care?
No. Housekeeping focuses on domestic tasks — cleaning, laundry, shopping and organisation. Personal care is a separate pathway; we can pair both under the same CQC-registered provider if needed.
What domestic tasks can visits include?
General cleaning, laundry and ironing, shopping or errands, kitchen tidy, changing beds and light organisation — tailored after assessment to what keeps the home manageable.
How often can housekeeping visits be scheduled?
Weekly, fortnightly or more frequent slots depending on the size of the home and what needs doing. We agree a reliable schedule you can plan around.
Are housekeeping staff DBS-checked?
Yes. Visits are delivered by vetted Caretaz team members under our CQC-registered Nottingham service — the same standards of recruitment and supervision as our care pathways.
