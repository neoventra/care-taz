About this service

Visits that fight loneliness — gently.

Companionship care from Caretaz Healthcare is for people who need more than an empty afternoon. Our carers bring conversation, shared hobbies, short outings and mealtime company — warm social support that lifts mood without turning the visit into heavy clinical care.

Loneliness can quietly erode confidence. Regular visits with consistent faces restore dignity, routine and something to look forward to — whether that is a walk to the shops, a favourite game or simply someone to talk to over a cup of tea.

Where personal care is also needed, companionship can sit alongside hourly care under the same Nottingham team — so wellbeing and practical help stay joined up.

Good to know

Questions families ask first

Straight answers before you book.

What does companionship care include?
Conversation, shared hobbies or games, short outings, mealtime company, welfare checks and gentle prompts with daily routines — focused on social connection rather than heavy clinical care.
Is companionship care the same as personal care?
No. Companionship centres on company and wellbeing. If personal care is also needed, we can combine visits or add hourly care under the same CQC-registered provider.
How often can companionship visits take place?
As often as feels right — from a weekly social call to several regular visits. We match faces and schedules so relationships can grow consistently.
Is companionship care CQC-regulated?
Yes. Companionship visits from Caretaz are delivered under our CQC registration by vetted, trained team members across Nottingham.
