Living with Parkinson's at home

Support that respects good hours and harder ones

Parkinson's rarely follows a tidy timetable. Tremor, stiffness, freezing and fatigue can swing within a single morning. Rigid care rotas that ignore that reality leave people rushed, frustrated or missing the window when medication works best.

Caretaz Healthcare designs Parkinson's packages around your real patterns — preferred wake times, dose schedules agreed with your specialist nurse, and how you like transfers, dressing and meals handled when movement is difficult. Based on Mansfield Road and regulated by the CQC, we document visits clearly so clinics and family stay informed.

Whether you need short visiting calls across West Bridgford or denser cover after a hospital stay, we keep the same trained carers where possible — so you are not re-explaining Parkinson's every time the doorbell goes.

Questions answered

Parkinson's care — frequently asked

How do carers adapt to Parkinson's on/off fluctuations?
Care plans note typical patterns and triggers. Carers are trained to slow down on stiffer or freeze-prone mornings, protect dignity during tremor, and never rush tasks when mobility dips — adjusting the visit rather than forcing a fixed checklist.
Can you support strict Parkinson's medication timing?
Yes. We align visit windows with prescribed times where possible, prompt doses as agreed with your pharmacy or specialist nurse, and keep clear records so GPs and Parkinson's clinics can see what happened at home.
Is Parkinson's care CQC-regulated at Caretaz?
Yes. Caretaz Healthcare is registered with the Care Quality Commission. Parkinson's support is delivered under the same recruitment, training and safeguarding standards as all our Nottingham home care.
Can Parkinson's care increase as the condition progresses?
Absolutely. Many families start with timed visiting calls and later add overnight, 24-hour or live-in cover. We review regularly so routines and familiar carers stay consistent as needs change.
