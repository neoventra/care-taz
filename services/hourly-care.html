About this service

Visiting care, built around real routines.

Hourly home care from Caretaz Healthcare is for people who want reliable help with daily living — without a live-in arrangement. Visits can be as light as a couple of hours a week or as frequent as several calls each day.

Our carers assist with personal care, meals, medication prompts, mobility and companionship. As needs change, we increase, reduce or reshuffle visits so support always feels proportionate.

It’s the ideal entry point for families balancing work and care, and for adults who value staying independent at home with the right help at the right time.

Good to know

Questions families ask first

Straight answers before you book.

How many hours of care can I book?
Packages range from a few hours a week to multiple visits each day. We design the schedule after assessment so it fits routines, not the other way around.
Can hourly care include personal care?
Yes. Personal care, dressing, meals, medication prompts, mobility help and companionship can all form part of a visiting package.
What if our needs change?
We review regularly and can increase, reduce or rearrange visits — including stepping into overnight or 24-hour care if required.
Is hourly care regulated?
Yes. All Caretaz visiting care is delivered under our CQC registration with trained, vetted carers.
