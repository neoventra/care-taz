Home Care Agency in Nottingham

Caretaz Healthcare: trusted home care that helps adults live safely, independently and with dignity.

Caretaz Healthcare is a specialist home and elderly care provider in Nottingham, trusted by local families for professional domiciliary care delivered with warmth. We support adults of all ages — particularly older people and those with long-term conditions — to stay safe, comfortable and independent at home.

Every visit is built around the individual. Our care teams take time to understand each person's needs and daily rhythms, so support feels personal rather than routine, whether that's a short daily visit or round-the-clock care.

Reliability is at the heart of what we do. Our carers are friendly, fully trained and genuinely committed — the kind of people families trust with someone they love.

Our ethos is simple: care should never diminish a person's independence, choice, rights or inclusion.
