About this service

Care that protects comfort and humanity.

Palliative and end-of-life care from Caretaz centres on comfort, dignity and emotional support — for the person and for the family around them.

We deliver calm personal care, comfort routines and sensitive communication, coordinating with healthcare professionals so wishes guide every decision.

The aim is quality of life: making each day as comfortable, peaceful and person-centred as possible at home.

Good to know

Questions families ask first

Straight answers before you book.

Is palliative care only for the final days?
No. Palliative support can begin earlier to improve comfort and quality of life, then intensify as needs change.
Do you work with hospice or district nurses?
Yes. We coordinate with clinical teams so home care complements medical support.
Can care include overnight presence?
Yes. Many families add overnight or continuous cover so someone trusted is always near.
How do you support families emotionally?
With honest communication, space to be present, and practical help that lifts the load of daily care tasks.
